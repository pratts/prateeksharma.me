I'm Prateek Sharma, a backend and systems engineer with 10+ years of experience.
I build services and infrastructure where correctness, reliability, and scale
matter more than surface area: API design, data modelling, workflow
orchestration, and the observability to keep it all honest.

## What I work on

Backend systems, distributed systems, databases, and the infrastructure around
them. Concretely, that has meant:

- **Data & integrations:** parsing and reconciling on-chain data across several
  blockchains, multi-tenant PostgreSQL schema design, full-text search, and
  Excel/S3 ingestion pipelines.
- **Fault-tolerant workflows:** Temporal-orchestrated, idempotent sync jobs with
  reconciliation and validation instead of best-effort scripts.
- **Real-time systems:** poker game and tournament engines handling thousands of
  concurrent players with event-driven, low-latency backends.
- **Platform work:** migrations (PHP to Node.js, services to Django, serverless
  booking engines), RBAC and identity, and observability with OpenTelemetry.

## What I care about technically

Systems that fail loudly and recover cleanly. Schemas that make illegal states
hard to represent. APIs that are boring to use. Enough observability to answer
"what happened?" without redeploying. Libraries small enough to understand in one
sitting.

## Background

I started in September 2014 as a graduate trainee at Adda52, a real-money poker
platform, and have since worked in transport/travel tech, B2B SaaS, and
crypto/fintech, on teams from 4 to 40+ people. Along the way I've worked across
Java, Node.js/TypeScript, and Python, and more recently Go, with PostgreSQL,
Redis, AWS, Docker, and Kubernetes underneath.
