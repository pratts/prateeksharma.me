Mind Sports League ran an online poker platform. I led development of the game
and tournament features on top of the existing platform.

## Engineering areas

### Tournament features

- Built advanced tournament mechanics: **hand-for-hand** play, **re-entry**, and
  **table balancing**.

### Real-time backend

- Engineered game-state management and an **event-driven backend** handling
  thousands of concurrent players with low-latency communication.

### Training tools

- Built a **poker simulator** integrated with an educational platform, so
  players could practise strategy and review analytics.

## Stack

Real-time / event-driven backend · game-state management · AngularJS (simulator)
